# The Pro Punter rule set

This is the default preset in Bet Tracker: the author's own system for growing a small bankroll toward a fixed goal. It's opinionated on purpose, so change anything under **Rules** to suit how you bet.

| Rule | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Max odds per bet | **1.25** | Only short-priced, high-probability selections. Compounding works better with frequent small wins than occasional big ones. |
| Max bets per day | **3** | Fewer, better bets. Stops a bad day from snowballing. |
| Max stake % | none | The daily quota decides the stake. Add a cap (for example 60%) if you want a protected reserve. |
| Max legs in a multi | **4** | Every extra leg multiplies the risk. Two or three legs are preferred. |
| Checklist | **required** | Every bet goes through the research steps first. |

## Allowed sports and leagues

- **Basketball:** NBA, NBL
- **Soccer:** major European leagues and cups, Saudi Pro League, A-League, MLS, international friendlies
- **AFL:** AFL
- **Cricket:** all
- **Motorsport:** Formula 1

Other soccer regions (South America, Africa, other Asian leagues) are off by default because team news and lineups are harder to verify there.

## Bans

- **Teams:** Cleveland Cavaliers, Arsenal (late-game swings make them unpredictable at short odds)
- **Players:** Cade Cunningham, De'Aaron Fox
- **Markets:** soccer match result (1X2), since double chance covers two of the three outcomes; and halves/quarters markets

## Checklists

Each sport has its own pre-bet steps:

- **Basketball:** official injury report and late scratches, back-to-backs and rest, home/away splits and form, pace and matchup, late news within 1–2 hours of tip-off, odds recheck.
- **Soccer:** head-to-head (including at the venue), form and goals/corners per game, injuries and rotation (3 games in 7 days is a flag), confirmed XI, xG, odds recheck.
- **Cricket:** pitch report, toss and dew, venue, bowler vs batter matchups, odds recheck.
- **AFL:** ins and outs, interstate travel, weather, form, odds recheck.
- **F1:** FP2 long runs, weather, grid penalties, odds recheck.

## Habits the app can't enforce

- If you have a feeling, run the checklist anyway. If the data says no, it's no.
- Never chase a loss with a bigger stake.
- Rising odds on your pick mean something has changed, so find out what. Falling odds mean the market agrees.

## A word on the goal planner

Compounding toward a big goal on a short deadline needs a winning streak. The app shows you the maths honestly, including how many wins in a row an all-in path would need. Pick goals and deadlines you can lose without it hurting.
